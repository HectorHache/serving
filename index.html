Every claim has a page number

The developer publishes a 559 page Spanish master walkthrough. Every statement in this research points at the page it came from. Where the guide is silent, the document says so instead of guessing.

Three passes, one answer

longcat-2.5, Gemini and this pass researched the same game independently. Roughly thirty points of disagreement were logged and settled against the guide. The audit trail is published, not hidden.

Wrong where the others were right

The first pass claimed the game has no Modo Fácil difficulty mode. It does, on page 7. Both colleagues had it right. That correction is in the document rather than quietly fixed.

Built for building

Sections 12, 13, 17 and 19 are the parts worth reading if you are making your own game: the fixed-order crafting track, the growing hub, the 1 EXP clamp, and what to watch out for.
